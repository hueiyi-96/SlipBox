# SlipBox
Receipt &amp; Expense Tracker

## Project Overview
This is a receipt recorder that helps users save and find receipts, track spending by category, and keep supporting evidence.

## Problem?
People save receipts in different places across files, email inboxes, and phone galleries. Later, it takes time to find the original, work out what the purchase was for, and remember the GST amount they intended to claim.

## Target Users
- GST-registered self-employed users (e.g. sole traders and freelancers)
- Self-employed users who aren’t GST-registered
- Salaried employees

## V1 Scope
Record and find receipts, view spending by category, split mixed receipts, record intended GST claims where applicable, and export business records with the original files.

## Core Features
- Save a receipt: Take a photo or upload an image or PDF. Use an AI-assisted scan to prefill the merchant, purchase date, total, and receipt number when readable. Review or correct the details, fill in any missing fields, and choose a category before saving. Users can also enter the details manually and save the original file.
- Find an old receipt: Search by merchant or receipt number, narrow results by date, and open the original photo or PDF of the receipt.
- View spending by category: See category totals for a selected period and open a category to view its receipts.
- Record the business portion: Mark a receipt as personal, business, or mixed. For a mixed receipt, add the relevant business items and their amounts while keeping one original file. 
- Track intended GST claims: GST-registered users can record the GST shown on a business or mixed-use receipt separately from the amount they intend to claim. They can mark uncertain entries as **Needs review**.
- Export business records: Choose a date range and download a business expense summary with the original receipt files. The summary lists GST amounts needing review separately from amounts the user has confirmed.

- Save, search, and download your own receipts.

- Export a period summary with supporting receipts (Limited to 5 scans/mth)
  
# Premium (V2 scope)
